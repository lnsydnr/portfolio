PERSONAL LAB

Splunk Self-Study Lab

A personal monitoring environment for practicing ingestion, search, dashboards, and alerting.

Overview

Built a Splunk lab to turn raw activity into useful security signals. The work focused on writing searches that answer practical investigation questions and presenting the results clearly.

Focus areas

  • Log ingestion and source organization
  • SPL search development
  • Security dashboards and visualizations
  • Alert design for monitoring workflows

Outcome

Established a repeatable space to test searches, refine detection ideas, and build familiarity with the day-to-day mechanics of SIEM operations.
