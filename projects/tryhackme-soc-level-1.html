SELF-STUDY

TryHackMe — SOC Level 1 Path

A structured series of practical rooms covering the core workflow of a security operations center.

Overview

Worked through hands-on investigations that move from alert context to evidence and response. The path reinforced repeatable analysis habits across endpoint, network, and log data.

Log analysis and event correlation

Reviewed security telemetry to identify suspicious behavior, correlate related events, and establish the timeline of activity. This reinforced the habit of moving from raw alert data to a clearer understanding of what happened and why it matters.

SIEM operations and alert triage

Practiced triaging alerts in a SOC workflow, validating signal quality, and narrowing the investigation to the most relevant facts. The work emphasized separating noisy events from actionable incidents while keeping careful documentation of findings.

Threat intelligence research

Used threat context to understand indicators, attack patterns, and the broader risk profile behind suspicious activity. This helped connect the alert itself to a more complete picture of adversary behavior and likely intent.

Incident response workflows

Worked through the practical side of incident handling by confirming scope, documenting observations, and evaluating which next steps best fit the situation. The exercises built familiarity with a repeatable response process during live-style investigations.

Practice record

Documented findings and investigation steps while working through the path. Continue to the TryHackMe profile for the external activity record.
